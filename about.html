About Our Team

The Arguments is a New Zealand FIRST® LEGO® League team made up of motivated learners who enjoy building, problem-solving, and working together to take on new challenges.

We focus on creativity, teamwork, and improving every day — whether it's coding, designing mechanisms, or presenting our ideas.

Our goal is simple: have fun, grow our skills, help others, and compete with spirit and respect.
