Gracious Professionalism

Gracious Professionalism is at the heart of FIRST®. It means helping others, showing respect, and competing with kindness and fairness.

Our team shows Gracious Professionalism by:

  • 🤝 Supporting other teams during events
  • 💡 Sharing tips, strategies, and ideas
  • 😊 Being positive, encouraging, and respectful
  • 🌟 Doing our best while helping others do their best

We believe teamwork and kindness make competitions better for everyone.
