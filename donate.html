Support Knowledge & Discovery

Want to support people and organizations exploring history, archaeology, and learning? Here are some places you can donate safely:

  • Archaeological Institute of America – Supporting archaeological research worldwide.
  • The British Museum – Help preserve historical artifacts and exhibitions.
  • National Geographic Society – Fund exploration, archaeology, and science programs.
  • Lonely Planet Foundation – Supports cultural understanding and preservation.
  • The National Trust – Protecting historical and archaeological sites in the UK.

If you love history and want to help others explore it, these organizations are a great place to start.
